ARLINGTON, Va.— On Thursday, Hanwha Defense USA (HDUSA), Hanwha Systems Co (HSC), and U.S.-based collaborative autonomy company HavocAI announced they will jointly develop 200-foot autonomous surface vessels (ASV). This agreement pairs the leading global shipbuilding company with the most advanced collaborative autonomy technology on the market. Hanwha is currently the only shipbuilder with an operational shipyard in the United States to enter into a joint agreement with an autonomous vessels company.
